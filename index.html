About Neo x Cryptic

BUILT FOR
THE CYPHER

Neo builds dancers with freestyle confidence, battle awareness, Hip-Hop foundations, and the kind of presence people feel before the first count drops.

Technique meets identity. Confidence becomes visible.

Neo x Cryptic blends Hip-Hop foundations, freestyle development, battle understanding, and movement coaching into training that feels real, not performative. Instead of only teaching routines, the focus is transitions, texture, timing, control, and personal style.

Whether you are brand new or already stepping into battle spaces, the goal stays clear: build confidence through movement, sharpen your body control, and learn how to hold your own energy without needing validation from the room.
